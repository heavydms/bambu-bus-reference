Cable pins 5/6: buffer duty vs printer motion commands

X2D + AMS 2 Pro, AMS B slot 1, 2026-11-23. Pin 5 duty of the 10 kHz complementary pair, logged by the spare XIAO through a 10 kOhm passive tap. Dashed lines: first appearance of each printer motion command on the RS-485 bus.

Load (from MARK 2)

Plunge to 9 % as the AMS pushes filament in, hold ~31 %, then the AMS's own feed loop: drift up to ~65 %, burst back to ~38 %, every ~7.1 s with no new bus commands. Settles at 47 % loaded.

Unload (from MARK 1)

Flat 53 % loaded; dip to 47 % after stop_on_use; jump to 89 % within 0.3 s of the unload command; settles at 76 % unloaded.
